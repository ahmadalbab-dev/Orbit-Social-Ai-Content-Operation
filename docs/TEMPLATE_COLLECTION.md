# Editable template collection and social identity

## Included collection

Orbit includes 20 original image starter kits and 20 original video recipes, alongside the existing five image frame families, five video frame/title families and three uploaded-photo arrangements. Counts describe editable recipes, not 40 new rendering engines or downloaded third-party templates.

Image kits apply an actual canvas ratio, photo frame family, background/ink palette, brightness/contrast/saturation, headline placement/size and an editable starter caption. Customer captions and uploaded media are preserved. Video recipes apply the actual export format, colour adjustments, fixed playback speed, title and/or timed starter captions when the customer has not already supplied them. The photo sequence recipe arranges uploaded overlays; it does not invent video clips. Preview and export retain the existing renderer. New uploads still start neutral without a news overlay or template title. Presets retain the canonical template families accepted by the AI current-state validator.

Image collection: Quiet luxury, Night launch, Summer drop, Soft journal, Studio drop, UGC cover, Café special, Beauty edit, Tech arrival, Weekend guide, Workshop invite, Fitness focus, Recipe note, Travel postcard, Mono manifesto, Community quote, Creator checklist, Launch countdown, Thank you note, Portfolio cover.

Video collection: Product showcase, UGC story, Photo sequence, Editorial film, Product reel, Quick demo, Creator tips, Travel diary, Mono story, Café moment, Event recap, Shop arrival, Slow detail, Tutorial steps, Fitness highlight, Portfolio film, Launch teaser, Customer note, Weekend cut, Behind the scenes.

## Research and reuse boundary (21 October 2026)

Current Adobe Express catalogs cover social video, Instagram/mobile/vertical video, posts and marketing designs. Those categories informed the original recipes above; no Adobe, Canva or CapCut template files, stock media or artwork were copied. A free template available in another editor is not automatically licensed for redistribution inside Orbit. Canva restricts Pro content in externally distributed templates. Third-party template packs require a verified redistribution license and supported editable format.

## TikTok identity

The generic hand-drawn monochrome note is replaced with the unmodified official circular colour icon from TikTok's developer asset pack. It stays legible in light/dark themes without recolouring or inverting the artwork. It identifies the channel; it does not assert endorsement, approved app review or live publishing.

Source: https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/getting-started-design-guidelines
Official asset ZIP: https://sf16-va.tiktokcdn.com/obj/eden-va2/uvzhqeh7nuhd/tt4d/logo-pack.zip
Archive file: `Dev Portal Logo Pack/TikTok Logo Pack/TikTok – Icons/TikTok_Icon_Black_Circle.png`.
Repository asset: `apps/web/public/social/tiktok.png` (original bytes, 1200×1200).

TikTok's developer page requires adherence to brand/use guidelines and states written permission is required for logo use. Availability in its developer pack is not represented as a separate permission grant. No logo was generated by AI or traced from an unofficial reconstruction.

TikTok appears in the existing hero channel row, Content Studio channel selector, Home example tabs, Calendar filters and Channels card/dialog. The Home example is labelled illustrative and not published. Existing default draft channels remain Facebook/Instagram/Threads until the customer selects TikTok. OAuth configuration and code are unchanged by this visual/template patch.
