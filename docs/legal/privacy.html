Orbit Social Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Orbit Social processes account identifiers, content, schedules, analytics and OAuth credentials only to provide content operations requested by an authorized workspace member.

Security

Social passwords are never requested. OAuth credentials are obtained through official provider consent screens, encrypted at rest, access-controlled by workspace, and removed when a connection is revoked or a verified deletion request is completed.

Sharing and retention

Data is shared with a social platform only to perform an authorized action. Operational records are retained only as needed for the service, security, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.
